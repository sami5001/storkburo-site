Privacy Policy

StorkBuro does not collect personal data.

StorkBuro is an offline-first iPhone and iPad app. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no StorkBuro-operated backend service in version 1.0. Your pregnancy details and scanned documents stay on your device and in your own iCloud account; another person sees them only when you invite them through partner sharing.

App privacy summary

Accounts

No StorkBuro account and no separate sign-in. The App Store purchase and iCloud access are handled by Apple.

Tracking

No tracking. No advertising identifiers. No cross-app tracking.

Analytics

No analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Sentry). No usage telemetry leaves your device.

Network

Content is bundled in the app. Apple uses network access for private iCloud sync and for pregnancy shares you choose to create or accept.

Why iCloud does not change this: your records sync through Apple's CloudKit in your private database or, only when you choose partner sharing, a participant-limited shared database. StorkBuro's developer does not operate a server and cannot access those records.

What StorkBuro stores on your device

StorkBuro uses Apple's Core Data framework to store the following locally:

  • Your display name and Bundesland
  • Pregnancy details: estimated due date, conception method, marital status, employment status, insurance type, multiple-birth and premature-birth flags, the baby's name, and (when entered) the actual date of birth or the outcome of the pregnancy, including a pregnancy loss
  • Checklist progress, notes, who a task is assigned to, and reminder dates
  • Custom and template-derived lists (e.g. hospital bag, baby gear)
  • Scanned document metadata and the document page images themselves
  • Appointment details (title, type, date, location, notes)
  • App preferences: language, appearance, notification toggles, biometric lock setting
  • If you turn on "Show Tasks in Spotlight" (off by default): task and appointment titles in the device's Spotlight index, which stays on the device

None of this information is sent to StorkBuro or a third-party analytics or advertising service.

iCloud sync and partner sharing

When iCloud is available for StorkBuro, Apple's CloudKit synchronizes your app data between your devices under your Apple ID in your private database. The developer cannot access it.

If you explicitly invite another person from Settings → Partner Sharing, Apple places the selected pregnancy and everything linked to it in a CloudKit share. Invited participants can view, add, edit, and delete its checklists, notes, appointments, lists, scanned documents, and document pages, including future items. Your local app profile and other pregnancies are outside that share. You can manage participants or stop sharing in the app. A participant may retain copies they exported or captured before access was revoked.

You can manage StorkBuro's iCloud access and stored data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Changes to iCloud data can synchronize to your other devices.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera — only when you tap "Scan document" in the vault. Images are processed on device.
  • Photo Library — only when you choose to import an existing photo into the vault.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on the document vault lock. While the lock is on, the app also asks for it before exporting a pregnancy PDF that contains scanned documents, and before turning the lock off.
  • Calendar (full access) — only if you add an appointment to your calendar. Full access is needed so the app can later update or remove the entry it created when you edit or delete the appointment; task deadlines are never written to your calendar.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in. Used for deadline reminders, appointment reminders, and weekly pregnancy updates. All scheduled locally on the device.

You can revoke any of these at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy.

Website and support data

The public website storkburo.app is separate from the iOS app. It is a static site with no cookies, no forms, no advertising, and no analytics tracking. Your light/dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage only.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Like any web server, GitHub receives the technical data your browser sends with each request (IP address, time, requested page, browser type) and may keep it in server logs for security and operational purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, reliable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). GitHub processes this data in the United States under the safeguards described in GitHub's privacy statement, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We have no access to and do not evaluate these logs.

Email. If you email hello@storkburo.app or support@storkburo.app, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send and use it only to answer your support, feedback, legal, or content question (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where you are a customer, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Our mailboxes are hosted in the EU by Migadu (Migadu Mail GmbH, Switzerland).

Third-party services

The iOS app contains no third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs and makes no network requests of its own; iCloud sync and sharing are performed by Apple's CloudKit under your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy.

Outside the app, we rely on Apple (App Store distribution and payment), GitHub (website hosting), and Migadu (email) as described above. These providers process only the information needed for those services.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete a record from inside StorkBuro, delete the app, or erase device data. Synced data remains in your private iCloud until you remove it through StorkBuro or your iCloud storage settings. Shared records remain available to accepted participants until the owner removes them or stops the share.

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and to keep reasonable support records, then deleted.

Children's privacy

StorkBuro is built for adults navigating German parental bureaucracy. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15), to have it rectified (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Because the app stores your records only on your device and in your own iCloud, most of these rights you exercise yourself: delete a record in the app, delete the pregnancy including all its data from the loss-support screen or Settings, or delete the app. For support emails, write to support@storkburo.app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one for your place of residence or for our seat (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
