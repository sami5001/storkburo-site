Simple terms for using StorkBuro.

StorkBuro is an informational companion for German parental bureaucracy and pregnancy logistics. These terms keep the expectations clear.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Using StorkBuro

You may use StorkBuro for personal pregnancy planning, bureaucratic checklist tracking, and document storage. Do not copy, resell, scrape, or redistribute the app content or website content without permission.

Informational nature

StorkBuro is an informational companion. We work to keep the content useful and accurate, but German federal and state rules change. Deadlines, eligibility, office addresses, required documents, and benefit amounts can vary by Bundesland, by year, and by your individual situation.

Always verify time-sensitive details with the relevant office — your Krankenkasse, Standesamt, Jugendamt, Elterngeldstelle, Familienkasse, employer, or lawyer — before relying on them for a decision.

Not medical, legal, or tax advice

StorkBuro is not a medical service, legal service, tax service, or substitute for professional advice. Information about Mutterschutz, Elterngeld, Kindergeld, Steuer-IDs, or paternity recognition is general guidance, not legal advice tailored to your case.

For medical questions, talk to your midwife, doctor, or hospital. For legal or tax questions, talk to a qualified advisor. In a medical emergency, call 112.

Local data and iCloud

StorkBuro stores your pregnancy data, scanned documents, lists, and preferences on your device. Local data may be lost if you delete the app, erase device data, or switch devices without restoring an iOS backup.

When iCloud is available for StorkBuro, data synchronizes through your private iCloud database under your Apple ID. We never receive a copy.

Partner sharing lets an owner invite selected people to one pregnancy through Apple's CloudKit sharing. Accepting an invitation grants read-write access to all data linked to that pregnancy, including checklists, notes, appointments, lists, scanned documents, document pages, and future additions. The owner is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent before sharing sensitive information and for managing or revoking access. Revoking access cannot delete copies another participant already exported or captured.

StorkBuro may change, add, remove, or update features and content over time, including the bureaucratic checklists, the deadline anchors, and the pregnancy guides. We may also correct errors or update these terms when needed.
